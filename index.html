Our Story

Two sisters. One little kitchen.

We grew up in Green Cove Springs — a slow, sweet town in Clay County, Florida, the kind of place where everybody waves from their porch and the church bells still call folks to Sunday service. Our family is small but our roots run deep: faith, tradition, and the quiet kind of joy you find in handing somebody a jar candle and watching them smile.

Reid Candle started the way most good things do — at mama's kitchen table. Wax warming on the stove and us two girls deciding what each candle ought to smell like. Holly's the dreamer with the big ideas (she picks the scripture for every label). Hannah's still learning her letters, but she's already got the best nose in the family.

Every candle we pour carries a little piece of home. And every label carries a verse — because we believe a candle ought to do more than smell pretty. It ought to brighten every heart it lights.

— Holly & Hannah
